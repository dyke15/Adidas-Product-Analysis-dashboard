
# 📊 Adidas Sales Performance & Product Analysis

An executive Power BI dashboard designed to evaluate sales performance, revenue trends, and product-level dynamics for Adidas across multiple retail channels.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project delivers a comprehensive sales analytics solution to help stakeholders monitor revenue growth, track volume metrics, and optimize sales channel strategies. By leveraging interactive dashboards, key decision-makers can quickly identify top-performing product categories and sales distribution across In-Store, Online, and Outlet channels.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem & Objectives
Stakeholders required clear visibility into historical sales performance and channel efficiency to answer critical questions:
* **Product Performance:** Which product lines generate the highest volume and revenue?
* **Channel Comparison:** How do In-Store, Online, and Outlet channels perform relative to one another?
* **Growth Tracking:** What is the period-over-period percentage growth across key business metrics?

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Power BI Desktop:** Dashboard visual design and interactive filtering.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Custom measures for Time Intelligence (PoP growth, dynamic dynamic calculations).
* **Data Modeling:** Star schema architecture connecting transactional sales data, date dimension, and product hierarchies.

---

## 📐 Key Metrics & KPIs
* **Total Sales:** Overall revenue generated.
* **Total Units Sold:** Total volume distributed across product categories.
* **Average Selling Price (ASP):** Price stability across retail channels.
* **Period-over-Period (PoP) Growth:** Performance variance compared to prior periods.

---

## 💡 Key Insights & Business Impact
* **Strong Volume Growth:** Total units sold reached **245K**, marking an **8.66% increase** compared to the previous period.
* **Revenue Expansion:** Overall sales grew by **10.97%**, driven by consistent pricing across major lines.
* **Channel Dominance:** **In-Store sales** consistently outperformed both Online and Outlet channels, highlighting strong retail footprint engagement.
